Let your booked SATS classes run your home. Add each SATS member in the household as a device and use Flows to act before a class starts, while you are away or when it ends: turn on the car's engine heater an hour before a morning class, but only when the centre is more than a few kilometres away; open the curtains and turn the lights up slowly before an early class; start the robot vacuum or lower the heating while you train; or switch on the sauna or the bathroom floor heating when the class ends, so it is warm when you get home. A dashboard widget shows the coming week's classes for the whole household, for example on a kitchen tablet.

This is an unofficial app, not affiliated with, endorsed by or supported by SATS. You log in once with your own SATS account while adding the device; the app keeps only a login token on your Homey and discards the password. It reads your bookings about every 30 minutes and cannot book or cancel classes. It uses the same private API as the SATS mobile app, which SATS can change at any time, so use it with your own account and at your own risk.
