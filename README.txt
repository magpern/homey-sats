Let your home react to your booked SATS classes. Add each SATS member in the household as a device and use Flows to act before a class starts or when it ends, for example to open the bedroom curtains an hour before a morning class. You can also get notified when a class is cancelled or gets a new instructor, or when you move from the waiting list to booked. The device shows your next booked class, your next waiting-list class and your best waiting-list position, and a dashboard widget lists the coming week's classes for all members.

This is an unofficial app, not affiliated with, endorsed by or supported by SATS. You log in once with your own SATS account while adding the device; the app keeps only a login token on your Homey and discards the password. It reads your bookings about every 30 minutes and cannot book or cancel classes. It uses the same private API as the SATS mobile app, which SATS can change at any time, so use it with your own account and at your own risk.
